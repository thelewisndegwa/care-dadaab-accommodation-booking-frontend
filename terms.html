Terms & Conditions

Please read these terms before submitting an accommodation booking request for the CARE Kenya Dadaab facility.

1. Purpose

This system is provided for requesting accommodation at CARE Kenya’s Dadaab facility. Submitting a booking request does not guarantee approval or room assignment.

2. Eligibility

Accommodation is intended for authorised CARE staff, consultants, partner organisation personnel, and approved visitors as determined by CARE Kenya.

3. Booking requests

All booking requests are subject to review by an Accommodation Officer. You must provide accurate contact and travel information. False or incomplete information may result in rejection.

4. Booking reference

After submission you will receive a unique booking reference. Keep this reference safe. You will need it to track your booking, contact CARE, or request cancellation.

5. Approval and room assignment

Approval and room assignment occur together. An approved booking always includes an assigned room. Room assignment is based on availability and operational requirements.

6. Cancellation

Guests cannot cancel bookings directly. You may request cancellation through the Track Booking page. An Accommodation Officer will approve or decline the request.

7. Check-in and check-out

Guests must check in and check out according to facility procedures. Assigned rooms must be left in good condition.

8. Conduct

Guests must follow CARE Kenya site rules, security requirements, and local regulations while staying at the facility.

9. Liability

CARE Kenya is not responsible for personal belongings left unattended. Guests stay at the facility at their own risk subject to applicable law and CARE policies.

10. Changes

CARE Kenya may update these terms as needed. Continued use of the booking system constitutes acceptance of the current terms.
