How it works

Simple steps from request to check-out. No guest account is required.

  1. Submit your accommodation request with arrival and departure dates.
  2. Save your booking reference — you will need it to track your booking.
  3. An Accommodation Officer reviews your request and assigns a room if approved.
  4. Check in on arrival and check out when your stay ends.

Before you book

Have your contact details, organisation, and travel dates ready. Review the terms if this is your first visit.
